The Reskin Deck

Every component available in the template, live in three color presets. The layout, spacing and behavior never change — only the palette does, which is the whole idea: one small edit in app/globals.css and a client's site looks built for them.

Section themes

Most pages stay on the dark root throughout. A section can flip to light on its own — commonly used for the contact form, to break up a long dark page — and every primitive inside re-themes for free.

How to reskin this for a new client: open app/globals.css and edit the nine values under :root plus the five under [data-theme="light"]. That's the whole rebrand — radius, spacing, motion and the type scale stay untouched, so every site you ship keeps the same quality bar regardless of which nine hex codes it starts from.

--color-background: #0a1414;
--color-surface: #10201f;
--color-text: #eef4f3;
--color-text-secondary: #93a6a3;
--color-accent: #22c3b6;
--color-accent-strong: #0b6b6a;
--color-border: rgb(238 244 243 / 8%);
--color-error: #ff6b6b;
--color-success: #34d399;
